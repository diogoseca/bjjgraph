<section id="overview" class="system-overview">

The Lockdown Blueprint covers Tom DeBlass’s lockdown half guard from both sides of the position. Electric-chair attacks sit alongside reversals, back entries and leg locks. Recovery and top-side escapes broaden the course beyond attacking from an already established lockdown.

</section>

## Is this for you?

- You want more offense and recovery options from lockdown half guard.
- You also want coverage of escaping an opponent’s lockdown.


### Consider an alternative if

- You need a general half-guard course spanning many styles.

<section id="coverage" class="system-section">

## Coverage and limits

- Lockdown entries, electric-chair attacks, reversals and back entries.
- Deep-half connections, leg locks and waiter-sweep material.
- Recovery, reverse lockdown and escapes from top.


### Limits

- The four volumes stay centered on lockdown; the electric chair is one attack within that position.

</section>
