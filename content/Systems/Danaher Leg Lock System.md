<section id="overview" class="system-overview">

John Danaher develops leg attacks around gaining inside position, controlling the knee line and moving between ashi configurations. The course covers inside and outside heel hooks, toe holds and the problems created by an opponent’s rotation or leg positioning.

Its eight volumes combine control and finishing material with entries from standing exchanges and guard. That breadth suits a grappler building a connected leg-attack game beyond a single ankle-lock finish.

</section>

<section id="coverage" class="system-section">

## Coverage and limits

- Gripping, inside position, knee-line recovery and single-leg, double-leg and cross-ashi configurations.
- Rotational resistance, toe holds, leg switches and entries from standing, half guard and other ground positions.


### Limits

- Heel hooks and rotational control occupy substantial parts of the course; it is broader than an ankle-lock-only instructional.
