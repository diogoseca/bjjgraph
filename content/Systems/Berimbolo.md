<section id="overview" class="system-overview">

The Berimbolo System Part 1: The Foundation introduces Mikey Musumeci’s approach through double-pull positioning, De La Riva grips and inversion. It then develops the basic berimbolo into stacking, pulling and uninverting options. This guide emphasizes the starting positions and foundations within that eight-volume release.

</section>

<section id="fit" class="system-section">

## Is this for you?

- You want a berimbolo course that includes the double-pull and grip-fighting context.
- You are interested in inversion-based guard attacks toward the back or mount.


### Consider an alternative if

- You want guard offense that avoids inversion and double-pull situations.

<section id="coverage" class="system-section">

## Coverage and limits

- Double-pull positioning, De La Riva hand fighting and inversion.
- Basic berimbolo entries with leg or hip grips.
- Stacking, pulling and uninverting options leading toward back control or mount.


### Limits

- This is Part 1: The Foundation, not the entire multi-release Berimbolo System. Collar and pant grips feature in its starting-position material.
