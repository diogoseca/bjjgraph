<section id="overview" class="system-overview">

Champions Stay Present is Josh McKinney’s course on the mental side of BJJ competition. The advertised material addresses nerves and self-doubt, pre-competition routines and regaining focus during a match, including when behind or under pressure.

Its six volumes span goals and training camp, over-training, competitors’ perspectives, competition day and post-event analysis. The emphasis is on the competitor’s attention and preparation, making it a different purchase from a technical course on positions or submissions.

</section>

## Is this for you?

- Nerves, self-doubt or losing focus are a bigger competition concern than adding another technique.
- You want a course that connects mental preparation with match-day focus and post-event review.

## Coverage and limits

- Managing nerves and self-doubt, pre-competition routines and mid-match refocusing, as advertised on the official page.
- Goal setting, training-camp preparation and discussion of over-training.
- Competition-day mindset and post-competition analysis.


### Limits

- The subject is competitor mindset; technique instruction and detailed event rules are separate needs.
