## Competition Mindset: Champions Stay Present

**Locate preparation, competition-day focus and performance review in Josh McKinney’s mindset syllabus.**


Being disappointed by a result and identifying a moment you lost focus are different review problems. Josh McKinney’s Champions Stay Present separates goal setting and camp preparation from competition day and post-event analysis. Use that structure to choose the part of the course that matches the experience you want to examine.

</section>

<section id="fit" class="content-section">

## Is this useful for you?

- Your competition notes record results but say little about what held your attention during the match.


### Consider an alternative if

- If the immediate problem is travel, packing or coach arrangements, Competition Preparation System has a more practical logistics focus.


</section>

<section id="start-here" class="content-section">

## Start here: Separate attention during the match from hindsight

Choose one competition you remember clearly. In one column record what occupied your attention before and during the match; in another record what you concluded after seeing the result. Identify a mismatch you want to investigate. The outline places event-day material in Volume 5 and retrospective analysis in Volume 6; owners can compare those two lessons against that specific example.

Published section: Volume 5: Compete Like you train: Navigating Competition Day. Volume 6: Let’s do that again: Post Competition Analysis.
