<section id="overview" class="system-overview">

The Ultimate Rubber Guard is built around Eddie Bravo’s rubber-guard approach, with dedicated volumes on its evolution and positional material. The publisher names mission control, chill dog, dead orchard and double bagger among the positions covered. An omoplata section and later variations complement the historical discussion.
