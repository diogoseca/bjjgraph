<section id="overview" class="system-overview">

The Single Leg Takedown follows Jimmy Sheptock’s wrestling approach from closing distance and clearing ties to finishing the captured leg. High finishes, low finishes and responses to the whizzer give it more depth than a course about the initial shot alone. Its setting is wrestling, which matters when choosing it for a jiu-jitsu takedown game.

</section>

<span id="is-this-useful-for-you"></span><span id="start-here"></span><span id="study-this-system"></span>
<section id="fit" class="system-section">

## Is this for you?

- You want wrestling detail on tie clearing and head-inside single legs.
- You need more finishing options when the defender applies a whizzer or the exchange drops low.


### Consider an alternative if

- Your main need is submission defense or choosing a guard-passing position after the takedown.

## Coverage and limits

- Closing distance, head position and clearing collar, underhook, wrist and two-on-one ties.
- Head-inside single-leg finishes from high and low positions.
- Whizzer responses, high-crotch transitions, crackdowns and misdirection finishes.


### Limits

- The four-part course is a wrestling instructional; submissions and guard landings are not its advertised focus.
