## Ariel Tabak: Outside Passing and Finishing

**Follow Ariel Tabak’s outside-passing study from getting around the legs to the problem of finishing with upper-body control.**


Outside Passing Compendium by Ariel Tabak separates getting outside the legs from progressing afterward. It suits a passer who reaches the flank but still has questions about finishing. Its later grip-specific chapters also make equipment choice part of selecting a study route.

</section>

<section id="fit" class="content-section">

## Is this useful for you?

- You get outside the legs but need a focused study of the remaining finishing problems.


### Consider an alternative if

- For a pass organized around a torso connection, compare Lachlan Giles’s The Body Lock Pass.


</section>

## Start here: Find the gap between flanking and finishing

Compare the free outline’s Volume 1 chapters on getting outside the legs, progressing afterward, and reaching upper-body control. Place your last stalled attempt at one of those points. If you own the course, use the progression chapter as your first viewing target and record what new control it asks you to recognize.

## What the sources cover

- Getting outside the legs, high-pummel responses and switching sides.
- Leg traps with collar-and-pant or hip-and-pant grips, plus several finishing options.
- Cross-grip passing and sleeve-grip chapters.

<span id="common-obstacles"></span><span id="assessment-metrics"></span>

### Limits

- Several later sections explicitly use gi grips; no-gi readers should distinguish these from the earlier outside-position topics.
