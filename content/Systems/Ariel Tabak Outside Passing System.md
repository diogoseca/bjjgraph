<section id="overview" class="system-overview">

Outside Passing Compendium by Ariel Tabak focuses on the gap between getting around the legs and completing the pass. Its four volumes develop outside entries, upper-body control and finishing options, with leg-trap and cross-grip variations.

The course suits passers who can reach the flank but struggle to finish. Collar, trouser and sleeve grips give it a substantial gi-specific component.

</section>

<span id="is-this-useful-for-you"></span><span id="start-here"></span><span id="study-this-system"></span>
<section id="fit" class="system-section">

## Is this for you?

- You get around the legs but lose the pass before securing upper-body control.
- You want outside-passing options that include gi leg traps and cross-grip passing.

## Coverage and limits

- Outside entries, high-pummel responses, side changes and progression to upper-body control.
- Leg-trap finishes through leg drags, knee on belly, long steps and stacking.
- Lasso and reverse De La Riva responses, north-south passing and cross-grip bullfighters.


### Limits

- Several sections require collar, trouser or sleeve grips; the whole course does not transfer unchanged to no-gi.
