<section id="overview" class="system-overview">

Systematic Submission Dilemmas brings together Craig Jones’s triangle variations, leg-lock attacks and transitions between them. Entries from guard and top position sit alongside specific defensive problems and commentary on recorded rounds. Its focus is the connection between upper- and lower-body attacks within one course.

</section>

## Is this for you?

- You want to connect triangle attacks with leg entanglements.
- You want triangle entries from both guard and top positions, with troubleshooting beyond the initial entry.


### Consider an alternative if

- You want a short course devoted to a single triangle variation or one leg lock.

## Coverage and limits

- Front, rear, reverse and side triangles, including responses to the defensive problems named for each.
- Triangle entries from open guard, closed guard, knee shields and top positions.
- Transitions into 50/50, X-guard and ashi garami, plus commentary on recorded rounds.


### Limits

- The eight-volume course combines several submission families; it is not a general guard-retention, passing or back-control curriculum.

</section>
