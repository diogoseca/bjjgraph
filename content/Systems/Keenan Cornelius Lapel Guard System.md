<h1>Lapel Guard: What Stops the Entry</h1>
<div class="system-tags"><span>Guard System</span><span>Advanced</span></div>

<p class="system-reference-count">20 related references (techniques and positions)</p>

<section id="overview" class="system-overview">

A guard player can hold a lapel and still lack enough loose fabric to form the intended entanglement. Jiu Jitsu X’s article featuring Keenan Cornelius explains that distinction from the passer’s side. It also covers approaches outside the legs and foot control, showing how a passer can obstruct the setup before a lapel guard develops.

</section>

## Is this for you?

- You obtain a lapel grip but struggle to establish the guard around it.

## Coverage and limits

- Lapel slack and length as entry requirements.
- Outside-the-legs approaches and foot control as obstacles to establishing lapel guard.


### Limits

- The source focuses on denying entries. It does not provide a full worm- or squid-guard attacking curriculum.
