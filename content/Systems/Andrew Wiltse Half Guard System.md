## Half Guard: Recovery Before Attack

**A topic guide for separating the decision to recover another guard from the decision to keep attacking from half guard.**


Kesting’s article presents leaving half guard as a legitimate choice when that position is being shut down. This page uses that question to organize recovery study and compare focused half-guard resources.

</section>

<section id="fit" class="content-section">

## Is this useful for you?

- You keep selecting another half-guard attack when your actual question is whether to recover a different guard.

- For a source-indexed half-butterfly course companion, compare Brian Glick; for coming up from guard, compare Dante Leon.

## Start here: Separate changing guard from abandoning the exchange

Read Kesting’s reason for returning to closed guard. Write down one recent half-guard exchange in which you kept pursuing the same attack. Identify whether your unanswered question is how to attack or how to recover another guard; take that specific question to the relevant instruction rather than assuming a fixed sweep-to-back chain.
