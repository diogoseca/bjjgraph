## IBJJF Strategy: Check Your Event and Division

**Connect your tournament plan to IBJJF registration, uniform and division-specific rules.**


A tactical plan assumes the right division, permitted techniques and a uniform that passes inspection. IBJJF publishes those requirements across its rulebook, uniform guidance and event pages. Use this guide to assemble the applicable references before you decide which match options belong in your plan.

</section>

## Is this useful for you?

- You are entering an IBJJF event and need to check whether your registration and intended game fit its requirements.

## Start here: Build the rule sheet for your actual registration

Open your event’s registration page beside My First Championship. Record your entered age, belt and gi/no-gi division; copy the links to its weight chart and correction deadlines. Then locate that division in the rulebook’s illegal-technique table and check one attack already in your plan. Keep the uniform guidance alongside the sheet so equipment and technique questions do not get mixed together.

Published section: My First Championship: Register for the championship. Rule Book: Article 6, illegal-technique table; Article 8, Gis and Uniforms.

<section id="coverage" class="content-section">

## What the sources cover

- Finding the event’s weight chart, division information, correction deadlines and schedule.
- Checking uniform requirements and the rulebook’s age/belt restrictions on techniques.
- Locating the lack-of-combativeness provisions rather than assuming that holding any lead permits inactivity.

<span id="common-obstacles"></span><span id="assessment-metrics"></span>

### Limits

- The organizer’s event page supplies the actual dates and division details.
- This page is a checklist for consulting the rules, not a replacement for the illegal-technique table or its exceptions.
