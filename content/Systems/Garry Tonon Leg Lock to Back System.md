## Garry Tonon: Wrestling into Submission Attacks

**Explore Tonon’s wrestling and scramble course through its back-taking, leg-entry and front-headlock study routes.**


Shoot To Kill: Wrestle, Scramble, Submit is broader than a leg-lock-to-back chain. It organizes submission-grappling takedowns and the attacks that surround them.

</section>

## Is this useful for you?

- You want to study what follows a wrestling exchange in submission grappling.
