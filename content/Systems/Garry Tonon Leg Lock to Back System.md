<section id="overview" class="system-overview">

Shoot To Kill: Wrestle, Scramble, Submit covers Garry Tonon’s connections between wrestling and submission grappling. Singles, doubles and body locks sit alongside leg entanglements, front-headlock attacks and scrambling positions. Back takes and leg locks are part of this broader curriculum, which also includes demonstrations through drills and rolling footage.

</section>

- You want submission opportunities connected to wrestling exchanges.

## Coverage and limits

- Wrestling posture and setups, singles, doubles, trips and body-lock returns.
- Ashi entries, front-headlock attacks, kimuras and back takes from wrestling situations.
- Scramble rolls, drills and rolling footage.


### Limits

- Its eight volumes span standing exchanges and multiple submission families; leg-lock-to-back transitions are only part of the course.
