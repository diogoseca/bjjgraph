<section id="overview" class="system-overview">

Samurai Foot Sweeps & Leg Trips develops Kenta Iwamoto’s standing attacks from kouchi and kosoto into grip fighting and clinch throws. The course also covers shot defense, extending its scope beyond foot sweeps. It fits grapplers interested in attacks from upright ties and over-under exchanges.

</section>

<section id="fit" class="system-section">

## Is this for you?

- You want standing attacks built around foot sweeps and clinch trips.
- You want grip-fighting and shot-defense material alongside throws.

## Coverage and limits

- Kouchi and kosoto mechanics and attacking situations.
- Grip fighting, over-under throws and overhook or underhook clinches.
- Single-leg defense and rear-body-lock counters.


### Limits

- The five parts focus on standing exchanges, without a ground-control or submission curriculum.
