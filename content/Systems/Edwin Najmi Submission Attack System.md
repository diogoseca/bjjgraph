<section id="overview" class="system-overview">

Mission Submission brings together Edwin Najmi’s darce, triangle and Estima-lock attacks. Each family has its own section, with entries ranging from half guard and turtle to spider and lasso guard. The appeal is a selection of distinct attacks across positions, rather than a complete offense built around one hold.

</section>

## Is this for you?

- You want a course covering darces, triangles and Estima locks together.
- You are interested in both upper-body attacks and gi-guard leg attacks.


### Consider an alternative if

- You want every entry to be independent of gi grips.

<section id="coverage" class="system-section">

## Coverage and limits

- Darce entries from side control, half guard, turtle and single-leg defense.
- Triangle attacks from closed guard, knee shield and spider guard, plus inverted and flying variations.
- Estima locks involving leg drags, lasso and De La Riva guard.


### Limits

- The three parts cover these specific submission families, not a complete submission or positional-control curriculum.

</section>
