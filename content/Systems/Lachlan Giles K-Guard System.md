<section id="overview" class="system-overview">

No Gi Open Guard Volume 1: K Guard places K guard within Lachlan Giles’s wider open-guard approach. It covers entries against different opponent postures and from retention situations, then develops offense through backside 50/50, X guard and other positions. The course contains eight parts, including troubleshooting and narrated rolling.

</section>

## Coverage and limits

- K-guard positioning and entries against kneeling, combat-base and standing opponents.
- Retention entries, backside-50/50 heel-hook attacks, X-guard options and counter responses.


### Limits

- Leg entanglements and heel-hook offense form a substantial part of the course; its scope goes well beyond learning a single guard entry.
