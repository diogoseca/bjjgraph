## Lachlan Giles: K-Guard Entries and Offense

**A K-guard companion that separates kneeling, combat-base and standing entries from later offense and troubleshooting.**


The release title says Volume 1, but its player outline contains eight volumes. Early material locates K guard within a wider open-guard approach; later blocks separate entries by opponent posture and then develop offense.

</section>

<section id="fit" class="content-section">

## Is this useful for you?

- You want to compare K-guard entries against different opponent postures without jumping directly to the finishing sections.


### Consider an alternative if

- If passes around or under your legs are the main problem before offense begins, compare the Giles–Tabak retention guide.

## Start here: Choose the posture-specific entry block

Find your opponent’s posture in the outline: kneeling in volume 4, or combat base/standing in volume 5. Pair that block with the general entry discussion in volume 3. When reviewing an exchange, note the posture before the entry attempt and whether it changes; do not infer the entry mechanics from the chapter name.
