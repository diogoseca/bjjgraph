## Eduardo Telles: Octopus Guard in the Gi

**A gi-focused companion to Octopus With Pajamas, organized around its half-guard, closed-guard and reverse-half sweep and back-take entries.**


This single-volume course offers several ways into the octopus theme. Half guard, closed guard and reverse half are separate study choices; their appearance in one syllabus does not make them a prescribed chain.

</section>

<section id="fit" class="content-section">

## Is this useful for you?

- You want gi octopus material with an explicitly listed sleeve entry and several starting guards.


### Consider an alternative if

- For a no-gi syllabus with more headlock-response and turtle sections, compare Craig Jones’s Octopus Guard 2.0.


</section>

## Start here: Separate the starting guards

Use the free outline to make three headings: half guard, closed guard and reverse half. Place one sweep or back-take chapter beneath each. Choose the heading closest to a position you already study; if you have course access, begin with the half-guard sweep and compare the next elbow-post lesson without assuming what changes.

## What the sources cover

- Half-guard sweeps and back takes, butterfly variations, and closed-guard sleeve material.
- Later sections name counters to octopus and reverse-half options.

### Limits

- This single-volume gi survey covers several entries and counters; it offers less separately indexed headlock and turtle material than Craig Jones’s four-volume course.
