<section id="overview" class="system-overview">

Octopus With Pajamas presents Eduardo Telles’s gi approach in one volume. Sweeps and back takes recur across several starting guards, including a closed-guard sleeve entry. The course also covers counters using kata gatame and Ezekiel before closing with reverse-half options.

</section>

<section id="coverage" class="system-section">

## Coverage and limits

- Half-guard and butterfly sweeps, closed-guard sleeve entries, hip bumps and back takes.
- Counters to octopus and reverse-half sweeps and back takes.


### Limits

- Its single volume is concentrated on gi guard exchanges; headlock and turtle offense are not developed into separate volumes.

</section>
