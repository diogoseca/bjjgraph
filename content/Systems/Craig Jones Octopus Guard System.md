## Craig Jones: No-Gi Octopus Guard

**A course companion for choosing between Craig Jones’s half-guard, headlock-response and turtle material in Octopus Guard 2.0.**


The title covers more than one guard position: the outline moves from half guard and side control to headlock responses and turtle. Choose the section that matches your problem before treating the course as a single attack sequence.

## Is this useful for you?

- You want to investigate no-gi half-guard exchanges that lead into headlock or turtle situations.

## Start here: Locate your first lost position

Compare volume 1’s half-guard/side-control discussion with the later headlock and turtle headings. In a bottom-position exchange you can review, record whether the problem begins while a leg is still trapped or after a side pin is established. If you own the course, start with the half-guard comparison and check the instructor’s distinction before choosing a counter section.

## What the sources cover

- Half-guard versus side-control distinctions and crossface discussion in volume 1.
- Sweeping and D’Arce-related responses in volume 2; headlock and turtle branches continue in volumes 3–4.

<span id="common-obstacles"></span><span id="assessment-metrics"></span>

### Limits

- This no-gi release extends into headlock responses, buggy-choke material and turtle; it is broader than a compact sweep-and-back-take introduction.
- It does not offer the gi sleeve-entry emphasis of Telles’s Octopus With Pajamas.
