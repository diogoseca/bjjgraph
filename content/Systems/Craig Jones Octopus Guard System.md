<section id="overview" class="system-overview">

Octopus Guard 2.0 starts with half guard, side control and crossfaces, then expands into sweeps and D’Arce counters. Craig Jones devotes the later volumes to buggy chokes, front headlocks and turtle attacks, including crucifix and leg-attack material. This is a broad bottom-position course with an octopus theme.

</section>

## Is this for you?

- You want no-gi octopus material that continues into headlock and turtle exchanges.

<section id="coverage" class="system-section">

## Coverage and limits

- Half-guard and side-control distinctions, crossfaces, sweeps and reverse-side-control responses.
- D’Arce counters, buggy chokes, turtle overwrap attacks and crucifix material.


### Limits

- Much of the later course concerns headlocks and turtle, so the four volumes are not all guard entries and sweeps.
