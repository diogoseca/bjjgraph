<section id="overview" class="system-overview">

Essential Connections: Chest To Back - Behind The Elbows covers the work between getting behind an opponent and establishing rear mount. Giancarlo Bodoni addresses open and closed turtle, four-point positions, stand-ups and defensive rolls before developing rear-control attacks. That breadth is useful when an opponent keeps moving or rising before you can secure the back.

</section>

<section id="fit" class="system-section">

## Is this for you?

- You get behind turtle but struggle to establish hooks or keep the opponent down.
- You want coverage of stand-ups and rolling defenses as well as back finishing.

## Coverage and limits

- Turtle breakdowns, near- and far-hook insertion and body-triangle connections.
- Following turtle movement, four-point positions, stand-ups and Granby rolls.
- Standing rear-body-lock returns, rear-control hand fighting and strangles.


### Limits

- The focus is offense after getting behind the elbows; it is not a general guard-passing or front-facing takedown course.

</section>
