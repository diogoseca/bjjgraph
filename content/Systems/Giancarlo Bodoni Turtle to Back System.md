## Giancarlo Bodoni: Turtle, Rides and Rear Mount

**Locate Bodoni’s study of turtle breakdowns, hook insertion and keeping an opponent down.**


Chest To Back - Behind The Elbows connects turtle and four-point study with standing rear-body-lock material and rear-mount offense.

</section>

<section id="fit" class="content-section">

## Is this useful for you?

- You get behind a turtled opponent but need to choose between breakdown, hook or stand-up study.


### Consider an alternative if

- For a narrower focus on established back-control hand fighting, compare Danaher Straight Jacket System.
