## John Danaher: Straightjacket Back-Attack Study

**Use the straightjacket portion of John Danaher’s back-attack course to organize questions about hand fighting and control.**


This is a focused route through Back Attacks Enter The System. Start with the named limitations before treating arm control as a universal solution.

</section>

<section id="fit" class="content-section">

## Is this useful for you?

- You reach the back but want a structured study of the hand-fighting stage.


### Consider an alternative if

- If reaching the back is your main problem, use the broader Back Attack System guide to locate the entry volumes.


</section>
