<section id="overview" class="system-overview">

The Roger Gracie Closed Guard System is a focused gi course on maintaining closed guard and building offense from it. Roger covers collar and lapel chokes, arm locks, kimura and wrist-lock attacks, alongside a dedicated sweep section.

The sweep material includes scissor and hook sweeps, attacks against an opponent with both knees down and omoplata sweeps. This is a choice for someone who wants more from closed guard; it does not attempt to cover every gi guard or the whole standing and passing game.

</section>

## Is this for you?

- You use closed guard in the gi and want both submission attacks and ways to come on top.
- You want collar and lapel offense alongside guard maintenance.

<section id="coverage" class="system-section">

## Coverage and limits

- Closed-guard maintenance and collar/lapel chokes.
- Lapel arm locks, kimura and wrist-lock material.
- Scissor, hook and omoplata sweeps, plus options against an opponent with both knees down.


### Limits

- The course centers on closed guard; open-guard systems, takedowns and guard passing are outside its main scope.
- Competition legality still depends on the event and division.

</section>
