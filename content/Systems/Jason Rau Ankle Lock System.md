<section id="overview" class="system-overview">

Jason Rau starts Dynamic Ankle Locks with grips, leg positions and finishing mechanics, then addresses attacks against seated and kneeling opponents. Y-guard and butterfly-hook situations receive separate treatment, alongside belly-down finishes and Aoki options.

The final volume covers alternatives when the ankle lock fails, including heel hooks, a bear trap and back takes. The course centers on the ankle lock while carrying the attack into other outcomes.

</section>

## Coverage and limits

- Grips, leg positions, outside ashi, Y-guard and butterfly-hook ankle attacks.
- Seated and kneeling finishes, Aoki options, a heel-hook dilemma and secondary back takes.


### Limits

- The syllabus includes Aoki locks and heel hooks; it is not restricted to straight ankle locks.
