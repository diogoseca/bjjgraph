## Jason Rau: Dynamic Ankle Locks

**Jason Rau’s Dynamic Ankle Locks groups ankle-attack study by grips, leg positions, opponent posture and secondary options.**


An ankle-lock question can concern the initial connection, a change in the opponent’s posture, or the next option after the attack stalls. Rau’s three-volume outline separates those areas and names both submission and back-taking alternatives.
