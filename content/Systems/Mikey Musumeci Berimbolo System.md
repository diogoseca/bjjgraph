<section id="overview" class="system-overview">

The Berimbolo System Part 1: The Foundation develops the basic inversion into several ways of advancing position. Mikey Musumeci covers stacking and pulling variations, twister hooks, uninverting and top-bolo situations. This guide emphasizes those variations for someone interested in what follows the initial berimbolo entry.

</section>

## Is this for you?

- You want stacking and pulling variations beyond the basic berimbolo.
- You want coverage of twister hooks, uninverting and top-bolo situations.

## Coverage and limits

- Stacking and pulling variations, including combinations between them.
- Twister hooks, shoulder and collar controls, and uninverting to mount or the back.
- Top-bolo material and connections to passing or arm-triangle attacks.


### Limits
