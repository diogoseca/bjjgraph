## Mikey Musumeci: Berimbolo Branches

**Study the stacking and pulling branches in Musumeci’s Berimbolo System Part 1.**


After identifying the double-pull foundation, this route helps you organize the course’s different routes toward the back and its later top-position options.

## Start here: Compare the direction of two published branches

Place the stacking section in Volume 4 beside the pulling section in Volume 5. Note which branch name matches the movement you are trying to understand. Owners can then use the opening Volume 6 combination lesson to record when the instructor changes branches, without assuming every reaction requires that change.
