<section id="overview" class="system-overview">

Power Ride: A New Philosophy on Pinning by Craig Jones approaches top control through leg rides and upper-body controls, with a dedicated section on turtle riding.

The six-volume series covers entries from pins and passing positions, then submission follow-ups. It suits grapplers who want to hold and attack an opponent across several top positions, including when the opponent turns toward turtle.

</section>

<span id="is-this-useful-for-you"></span><span id="start-here"></span><span id="study-this-system"></span>
<section id="fit" class="system-section">

## Is this for you?

- You can get on top but struggle to keep an opponent down as they turn or build toward turtle.
- You want wrestling-style rides alongside upper-body pinning and submission options.

<section id="coverage" class="system-section">

## Coverage and limits

- Leg splitting, rides, turks, shelves and rear-mount control.
- Crossfaces, claws, handcuffs and half nelsons, plus turtle rides and cradles.
- Entries from pins and passes, followed by arm-triangle, darce, kimura and other submission topics.


### Limits

- Control across several rides and pins takes priority over a complete guard-passing curriculum or a mount-only treatment.
