<section id="overview" class="system-overview">

Basics To Advanced: Ankle Picks For Jiu Jitsu treats the ankle pick as both a standing takedown and a seated-guard attack. Aaron Benzrihem covers distance, grips and setups before exploring same-side and cross-body finishes. The range of starting positions makes this relevant to grapplers who want an ankle attack that connects their standing and guard games.

</section>

## Coverage and limits

- Distance, grip choices and standing setups involving feints, trips and collar drags.
- Seated entries, combat-base situations and three-point-stance attacks.
- Same-side and cross-body finishing variations.


### Limits

- This is an ankle-pick course, with gi-grip setups among its material; it does not cover the full range of wrestling takedowns.
