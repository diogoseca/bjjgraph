<section id="overview" class="system-overview">

Alex Enriquez brings stance and sprawl fundamentals together with defenses to specific wrestling attacks. The course addresses singles and doubles from space as well as situations where the opponent already holds an elevated leg. Ankle picks, fireman's carry and dogfight positions are also included, followed by material on head and hip position, overhooks and clearing underhooks. This is a focused option for grapplers whose problems begin on the feet.

</section>

## Is this for you?

- You struggle with wrestling shots or with recovering once an opponent has secured your leg.
- You want takedown defense that also addresses upper-body ties and their removal.

## Coverage and limits

- Stance, motion, sprawl and defensive principles, with stance-and-motion and head-blocking drills.
- Single- and double-leg defense, elevated-leg situations, ankle picks, low singles, fireman's carry and dogfight cases.
- Head and hip positioning, whizzer/overhook use, front-headlock throw defense and clearing single or double underhooks.


### Limits

- The curriculum is wrestling defense for grappling. Gi-specific collar-and-sleeve fighting and competition guard-pull strategy are outside its advertised scope.

</section>
