<section id="overview" class="system-overview">

The Front Headlock System by John Danaher connects entries and control with both submission and positional options. Its eight volumes cover standing, kneeling and seated exchanges, with a separate guard-passing section.

Alongside guillotines, anacondas and darces, the course includes go-behinds and cradles. Its breadth suits grapplers who want the front headlock to serve more than one finishing option.

</section>

## Is this for you?

- You want front-headlock entries and positional options as well as choke finishes.
- You use the front headlock in both standing exchanges and seated or passing situations.

## Coverage and limits

- Reactive entries, snapdowns and transitions from standing front headlock to the mat.
- Guillotine, anaconda and darce variations, cradles and go-behinds.
- Seated front headlocks, hip heists and applications against seated guard and half guard.


### Limits

- The course follows front-headlock exchanges; it is not a complete turtle-riding or general guard-passing course.
