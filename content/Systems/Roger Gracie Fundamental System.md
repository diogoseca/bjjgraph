<section id="overview" class="system-overview">

The Roger Gracie Closed Guard System develops offense from one guard. Roger Gracie combines collar and lapel attacks with arm locks, sweeps and routes to the back. This is a focused choice for gi players who want more options after securing closed guard.

</section>

<span id="is-this-useful-for-you"></span><span id="start-here"></span><span id="study-this-system"></span>
<section id="fit" class="system-section">

## Is this for you?

- You want closed-guard offense using collar and lapel grips.
- You want sweeps and back takes alongside submissions.


### Consider an alternative if

- You want a course that avoids gi grips.

<section id="coverage" class="system-section">

## Coverage and limits

- Guard maintenance, collar chokes and lapel attacks.
- Arm locks, kimuras and wrist locks.
- Scissor, hook and lumberjack sweeps, plus side-closed-guard back attacks.


### Limits

- The four parts cover closed guard, not a full fundamentals curriculum across passing, escapes and other guards.

</section>
