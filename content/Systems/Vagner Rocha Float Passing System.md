## Vagner Rocha: Float-Passing Exits

**Compare the float-position exits in Vagner Rocha’s middle volume while keeping its knee-cut and standing-passing material in context.**


Float Passing System by Vagner Rocha has three distinct parts: knee-cut passing, float passing and standing passing. The title can hide that broader scope. This guide centers the middle volume so readers interested in the float position can find that study directly.

</section>

## Start here: Compare two exits from the same named position

Use the public Volume 2 contents to locate the smash-pass and reverse-knee-slice sections immediately after the entry lesson. Note that both are presented from the float position, then choose which one answers your current question. If you own the course, watch the entry lesson first and compare the starting configurations shown in those two branches before assuming they are interchangeable.
