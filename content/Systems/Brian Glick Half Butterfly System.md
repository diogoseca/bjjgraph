## Brian Glick: Half Butterfly

**A companion to Under Pressure: Half Butterfly Mastery that separates entry problems, upper-body attacks and leg-entry study.**


The course devotes its first volume to positioning and entries, then splits attack material across two volumes. The fourth volume is an executive summary, useful as an index when returning to a specific topic.

</section>

<section id="fit" class="content-section">

## Is this useful for you?

- You want half-butterfly study that includes how to reach the position and how the course separates its attack families.


### Consider an alternative if

- If your starting point is fully seated butterfly rather than half butterfly, compare Marcelo Garcia’s Butterfly Guard guide.

## What the sources cover

- Inside position, head position and entries from several bottom positions.
- Sweeping and upper-body attacks in volume 2; near- and far-leg entries plus troubleshooting in volume 3.

### Limits

- Half butterfly is the organizing position; a separate executive summary repeats the main subjects rather than adding a fourth attack family.
