<section id="overview" class="system-overview">

Brian Glick builds Under Pressure: Half Butterfly Mastery around reaching and attacking from half butterfly. Positioning and entries lead into sumi gaeshi, shoulder-crunch and kimura material, followed by near- and far-leg entanglements. A fourth volume condenses the main material into an executive summary.

</section>

## Is this for you?

- You want half butterfly as an attacking guard, including both sweeping and leg-entry options.

<section id="coverage" class="system-section">

## Coverage and limits

- Inside and head position; entries from closed guard, seated butterfly, reverse De La Riva and pinned positions.
- Sumi gaeshi, shoulder crunch and kimura; near- and far-leg ashi entries and troubleshooting.


### Limits

- The attacks center on half butterfly. The entries from other positions do not make this a general seated-guard or pin-escape course.
