<section id="overview" class="system-overview">

Brian Glick devotes this seven-volume course to the problems that arise once an opponent controls your back. It combines defensive hand fighting with escapes from either side and from the body triangle, including routes to closed guard, half guard and ashi garami. Separate material addresses crucifix control, standing back control and strangle defenses at different stages.

</section>

<span id="is-this-useful-for-you"></span><span id="start-here"></span><span id="study-this-system"></span>
<section id="fit" class="system-section">

## Is this for you?

- You repeatedly get stuck in rear mount, especially when hooks, a body triangle or a trapped arm complicate the escape.
- You want back escapes and strangle defenses in the same course.

## Coverage and limits

- Defensive hand fighting, trapped-arm problems, elbow cuts, barrel rolls and folding or bridging.
- Overhook-side and underhook-side escapes, hook troubleshooting, body-triangle escapes and guard recovery.
- Special rear controls, including the crucifix and standing back control; early-, middle- and late-stage strangle defense.


### Limits

- The course concentrates on rear control. Mount and side-pin escapes are outside its main curriculum.

</section>
