<section id="overview" class="system-overview">

Dante Leon’s Wrestling Up From Guard focuses on rising into attacks from the bottom. Distance management and forcing reactions introduce material from X guard and De La Riva; later sections address reverse De La Riva, whizzers, snapdowns and butterfly attacks. Its three volumes connect wrestling to existing guard positions.

</section>

<section id="coverage" class="system-section">

## Coverage and limits

- Distance management, reactions and wrestling up from X guard and De La Riva.
- Reverse-De-La-Riva underhooks and whizzer responses; butterfly knee picks and arm drags.


### Limits

- The starting point is guard. This is not a general course on takedowns from a standing tie-up.
