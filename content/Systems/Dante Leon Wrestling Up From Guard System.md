## Dante Leon: Wrestling Up From Guard

**A study route through Dante Leon’s distance-management discussion and coming-up material from X, De La Riva, reverse De La Riva and butterfly guard.**


This course groups coming-up attacks by starting guard. Its opening distance discussion offers a common study question before the outline separates into X/De La Riva, reverse De La Riva and butterfly material.

</section>

<section id="fit" class="content-section">

## Is this useful for you?

- You want to study coming up from guard and need to choose a starting position rather than collect unrelated stand-ups.


### Consider an alternative if

- For reverse-De-La-Riva recovery and inversion branches beyond wrestling up, compare Leon’s dedicated reverse-De-La-Riva guide.


</section>

## Start here: Compare two reverse-De-La-Riva situations

Find the knee-down and squared-up opponent lessons in volume 2. Use those labels to sort two exchanges from your own notes or available footage. Record the opponent posture before the bottom player rises; keep observations separate from explanations of the mechanics.
