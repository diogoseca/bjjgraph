## John Danaher: Kimura Branches

**Use Danaher’s kimura course to compare changing submission with changing position.**


This companion organizes one connected attack family. The kimura is the common subject across dorsal, T-kimura, guard and takedown situations.

## Start here: Compare changing the attack with changing the position

Locate the two switching lessons in Volume 2. Make separate notes for the next submission and the next position, leaving their conditions blank. Owners can watch the lessons in order and fill those conditions from the demonstrations rather than assume a blocked kimura always has one prescribed follow-up.

Published section: Volume 2: Switching to Alternative Submission, 34:04–39:18; Switching to Different Position / System, 39:18–48:56
