## John Danaher: Back Entries and Attacks

**Navigate John Danaher’s back-attack course by distinguishing access to the back from control and finishing.**


The course spreads back control, auxiliary attacks and entries across separate volumes. Use that structure to locate the stage where your study currently stalls.

</section>

<section id="fit" class="content-section">

## Is this useful for you?

- You need to decide whether your back-attack study should begin with entries or established control.


### Consider an alternative if

- If you already reach the back and want hand-fighting study, choose Danaher Straight Jacket System.


</section>

## Start here: Locate the missing stage in a back-take exchange

Recall one exchange where you got behind someone but did not settle on the back. Compare that moment with the entry headings in Volumes 7–8 and the control headings in Volume 1. Choose which group describes your unresolved stage; owners can begin at the entry introduction below.
