## Lachlan Giles: Studying the Body Lock Pass

**Separate securing the body lock, clearing the knee line and finishing the pass in Lachlan Giles’s focused passing study.**


The Body Lock Pass by Lachlan Giles concentrates on one passing family across six volumes. Its official description separates entry, controlling the opponent, knee clearance and finishing, with narrated rolling. Use those distinctions to locate a sticking point instead of treating a secure lock as a completed pass.

## Start here: Locate the leg barrier after the hands connect

Read the linked public explanation, The Body Lock Pass, with Lachlan Giles. Mark the point where the hands connect, where the first knee clears, and where the remaining leg is freed. Compare those distinct events with your own study notes. The written explanation supplies the observation task; the embedded video has not been reviewed here.
