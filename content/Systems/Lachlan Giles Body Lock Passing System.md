<section id="overview" class="system-overview">

The Body Lock Pass by Lachlan Giles follows one passing family in depth across six volumes. It covers entering the body lock, pinning the opponent, clearing the knee line and finishing, alongside responses to counters and defenses.

The course also includes the Sao Paulo closed-guard break and narrated rolling. That combination suits passers who want both the mechanics of the body lock and examples of its use in sparring, rather than a collection of unrelated passes.

</section>

## Is this for you?

- You want to make body-lock passing a substantial part of your no-gi game.
- You can secure the lock but struggle to clear the legs or finish the pass.

## Coverage and limits

- Body-lock entries, pinning, knee clearance, finishes and responses to counters.
- Sao Paulo closed-guard opening within the body-lock approach.
- Narrated rolling accompanying the technical instruction.


### Limits

- The six volumes concentrate on the body lock; they are not a broad survey of outside passing and open-guard passing styles.
