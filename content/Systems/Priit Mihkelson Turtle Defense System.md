<section id="overview" class="system-overview">

Priit Mihkelson's course focuses on the turtle position, from neck and elbow positioning to dealing with hooks, harnesses and specific attacks. It includes turtle situations arising against over-under, toreando and knee-slide passing, as well as clock chokes, front-headlock submissions and crucifix threats. The final volume covers exits, including spin escapes, returns to guard, Granby movement and standing.

</section>

## Is this for you?

- You often end up in turtle during passing exchanges and want instruction on its defensive problems.
- You want a turtle course that includes both responses to attacks and ways to return to guard or standing.

## Coverage and limits

- Turtle posture, neck and elbow principles, pushing and pulling pressure, and hook management.
- Harnesses, clock chokes, passing situations, wrestling tilts, D'Arce, anaconda, guillotine and crucifix threats.
- Hip-pull and harness counters, spin escapes, guard returns, Granby movement and standing from turtle.


### Limits

- The course centers on turtle and transitions out of it; it is not a broad mount, side-control and rear-mount escape series.
