## Jonathan Thomas: Double Sleeve Guard

**A gi guard companion connecting the course’s double-sleeve control material with lasso/spider defenses and opponent-posture branches.**


Thomas’s outline crosses several leg configurations while retaining a double-sleeve theme. The useful comparison is between control lessons and the later posture-specific branches, rather than treating every listed sweep as available from one fixed position.

</section>

## Is this useful for you?

- You want to organize gi double-sleeve study around control losses or a standing-to-kneeling posture change.


### Consider an alternative if

- If your focus is gi single-leg-X entries and fabric-grip sweeps, compare Adam Wardzinski’s guide.

## Start here: Identify control loss before choosing a sweep

Compare the four named lasso/spider defense problems in volume 2 with the one-knee-up and kneeling sections in volume 4. Decide whether the exchange you want to study is a control-loss problem or an attack against a particular posture. Begin with basic control if you cannot yet distinguish the two.
