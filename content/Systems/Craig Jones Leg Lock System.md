<section id="overview" class="system-overview">

Down Under Leg Attacks covers Craig Jones’s entries and attacks across several leg entanglements. Heel exposure and finishing sit alongside common escape responses, while back takes provide another direction from leg-control positions. Its four volumes also connect leg attacks to existing submissions and guard entries.

</section>

## Is this for you?

- You want heel-hook material across several entanglements.
- You want escape-response and back-taking coverage alongside leg-lock finishes.

<section id="coverage" class="system-section">

## Coverage and limits

- Single-leg X, saddle and 50/50 positioning and attacks.
- Heel exposure, finishing and responses to running or kick-off escapes.
- Back takes and leg entries connected to guillotines, kimuras and guard positions.


### Limits

- The course emphasizes heel-hook offense and its positional connections; it is not an equal survey of every foot, ankle and knee submission.

</section>
