## No-Gi Competition: Compare the Rules First

**Compare IBJJF and ADCC scoring and division restrictions before choosing a no-gi match plan.**


No-gi describes attire, not a single ruleset. ADCC uses scoring phases and negative points, while IBJJF has its own scoring and penalty structure. Even within ADCC, the beginner/intermediate supplement changes permitted techniques and timing. Choose your division before importing tactics from a championship match.

</section>

## Start here: Check an opening choice in the right match phase

Select an actual division and identify the applicable rule document. Compare its opening phase with its scoring phase: when do positive and negative points begin, and what does it say about voluntarily going to the ground? Then look up one submission you already study in that division’s restrictions. Record the relevant passages beside your planned opening rather than assuming a professional match supplies permission.

Published section: ADCC: Time Limits; Penalties (Negative Points); Beginners & Intermediate illegal-technique table. IBJJF: Articles 2.5 and 6.

## What the sources cover

- Comparing scoring values: a qualifying mount scores four points in IBJJF and two in the cited ADCC championship rules.
- Checking when ADCC positive and negative points apply, plus the experience-category technique restrictions.
- Separating an event’s rule choices from a general preference for guards, wrestling or submissions.

### Limits

- The ADCC professional list does not describe all Open, Masters or beginner permissions.
- This comparison does not cover every no-gi promoter or submission-only overtime format.
