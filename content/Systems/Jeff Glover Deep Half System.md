## Jeff Glover: No-Gi Deep Half

**A deep-half companion that separates three named exit routes from entry study and upper-body troubleshooting.**


The outline introduces exits before surveying entries from several bottom positions. Its final volume separately addresses underhooks and chin straps, so a stalled deep-half exchange has a different study destination from a failed entry.

</section>

<section id="fit" class="content-section">

## Is this useful for you?

- You want to compare deep-half exits with the upper-body problems that appear in Glover’s troubleshooting volume.


### Consider an alternative if

- If half-butterfly entry and attack choices better match your starting position, compare Brian Glick’s guide.

## Start here: Separate entry trouble from control trouble

In the outline, compare the entry from the bottom position you encounter with volume 3’s underhook and chin-strap labels. Classify a recent stalled exchange as failure to enter or difficulty after entry. If you have access, study the relevant troubleshooting lesson before adding another entry to your notes.

## What the sources cover

- Overtop, backdoor and single-leg routes, followed by entries from knee shield and other bottom positions.
- A final troubleshooting and drill block, including underhook and chin-strap topics.

### Limits

- The entry collection is organized around reaching deep half; it is not a complete escape curriculum for every listed bottom position.
