<section id="overview" class="system-overview">

The No Gi Deep Half Guard 2.0 presents Jeff Glover’s overtop, backdoor and single-leg options alongside ways into deep half from knee shield and pinned positions. The final volume addresses underhooks, chin straps and opponents jumping over the head, then adds drills. It covers both reaching the position and dealing with resistance once there.

</section>

- You want deep half as a route out of bottom positions and need answers to upper-body control.

<section id="coverage" class="system-section">

## Coverage and limits

- Overtop, backdoor and single-leg options; entries from knee shield, mount and other bottom positions.
- Underhook and chin-strap problems, opponents jumping over the head and deep-half drills.


### Limits

- The escape material leads toward deep half; it does not offer a complete escape system for every starting pin.
