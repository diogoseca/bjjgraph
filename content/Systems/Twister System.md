## Twister and Truck: Choosing an Entry Study

**A source-based reading guide to Eddie Bravo’s announced Twister and truck-entry material from back and side-control contexts.**


The inspected announcements emphasize different starting contexts. Episode 85 describes Twister work from the back and side-control scrambles; episode 99 describes twister side control with back-taking and truck-entry material. These descriptions support choosing a topic, not reconstructing the movements.

</section>

## Is this useful for you?

- You want to separate Twister study from the question of how the truck is entered.


### Consider an alternative if

- If your immediate interest is positional movement from twister side control, start with the episode 99 announcement rather than episode 85’s Twister-focused description.

## Start here: Compare the announced starting contexts

Read the two short official announcements. Put back-position and scramble questions under episode 85, and twister-side-control entry questions under episode 99. Select the description closest to your current problem; do not fill in a lockdown-to-truck sequence that neither announcement explains.
