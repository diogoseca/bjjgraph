## Caio Terra: Guard-Recovery Lesson Map

**Caio Terra Online's defense archive offers specific guard-recovery lesson choices, including one-legged X against a toreando pass and half-guard recovery.**


Start with the guard and passing situation you can identify, then use the archive to find a matching lesson. These individual entries support a focused reading route; the public archive does not present them as a single named guard-retention system.

## Start here: Match the lesson to the guard you actually lost

Take one guard-passing exchange from your notes or footage and describe the guard at the last moment you can identify it. Compare that description with ONE LEGGED X VS TOREANDO PASS and HALF GUARD RECOVERY in the official archive. Select a matching entry only when its named situation fits; if neither does, keep the exchange as a separate problem rather than using a generic recovery label. The Q&A entry is an additional half-guard study destination for members.
