<section id="overview" class="system-overview">

Adam Wardzinski’s Single Leg X Reimagined connects butterfly, shin-to-shin and De La Riva entries to sweeping and submission options. Sleeve and lapel variations give it a distinct gi emphasis. The four volumes also cover technical stand-ups, back takes and responses to long-step and pressure passing.

</section>

## Coverage and limits

- Entries from butterfly, shin-to-shin and De La Riva; scissor, hook and overhead sweeps.
- Technical stand-ups, back takes, foot locks and counters to pulling and passing.


### Limits

- Several attacks depend on fabric grips, so this is not an exclusively no-gi single-leg-X course.
