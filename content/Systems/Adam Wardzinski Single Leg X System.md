## Adam Wardzinski: Single Leg X in the Gi

**A gi-specific single-leg-X companion that distinguishes the course’s entry choices, fabric-grip sweeps and responses to passing.**


The syllabus starts with several routes into the position and later separates sweeps, stand-up options and counters. Sleeve and lapel labels are a useful sign that some examples depend on gi connections.

</section>

<section id="fit" class="content-section">

## Is this useful for you?

- You already have a butterfly, shin-to-shin or De La Riva interest and want to find its single-leg-X entry in a gi syllabus.


### Consider an alternative if

- For a broader X-guard entry and sweep collection, compare The Marcelo X Guard.

## Start here: Match an entry to a grip-dependent follow-up

In volume 1, find the entry from the guard you currently study. Then compare volume 2’s far-sleeve and lapel headings with the other sweep labels. Mark which lessons explicitly require clothing grips so you do not mistake them for interchangeable no-gi examples.
