<section id="overview" class="content-section">

The attacker has a genuine leg entanglement with the knee line contained and the heel accessible to a controlled upper-body grip. Carni isolates a shoulder with a high leg. A heel-hook attack requires releasing or leaving that configuration and establishing separate hip, knee and heel control.

Confirm that the opponent's leg, rather than an arm or shoulder, is enclosed. Manage distance so the leg cannot move freely through the hold. Keep the knee connected to the entanglement without forcing sideways pressure in a drill.

Locate the heel lever without cranking the foot to expose it. Connect the grip with no finishing rotation; a secure heel catch is the stopping point in cooperative practice. Open the heel grip and leg connection before either partner changes direction.

Use only supervised capture-and-release practice with no rotational finish. Check the actual event division; no-gi clothing alone does not make heel hooks legal.
