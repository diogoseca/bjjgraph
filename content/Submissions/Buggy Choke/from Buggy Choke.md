<section id="overview" class="content-section">

The bottom player finishes from the established Buggy arm-and-leg loop beneath side control, using controlled choke pressure and releasing immediately at any signal.

Begin underneath side control with your same-side arm and leg forming the Buggy loop around the opponent's head and one arm. Confirm the head-and-one-arm enclosure before adding pressure; side-control contact alone is not the choke.

Keep the raised leg supported by a comfortable hip angle rather than pulling the foot against a fixed knee. Take up the choke pressure gradually with a cooperative partner and qualified supervision. An interrupted finish may leave the Buggy loop intact; complete loop loss returns you to Side Control/Bottom. Release immediately at a hand tap, foot tap, verbal signal or distress, then free the head and arm without forcing the knee.

Practice cooperatively with controlled movement. Stop at any tap, verbal signal, or distress; release all joint or neck pressure before changing direction. Do not force a trapped knee, shoulder, or neck to preserve the route.

<section id="safety-considerations" class="content-section">

## Safety Guide

**Injury Risks**:

| Injury | Severity | Recovery Time |
|--------|----------|---------------|
| Neck injury from excessive pressure | High | Variable; obtain qualified medical assessment if injured. |
| Neck or knee strain during a forced escape | High | Variable; do not train through symptoms. |


**Application Speed**: Apply gradually and release immediately at any signal.

**Tap Signals**:
- Hand tap
- Foot tap when the arms are trapped
- Verbal tap or stop


**Release Protocol**:
1. Stop adding pressure immediately.
2. Remove the joint or neck load before changing grips.
3. Free the trapped limb or neck and check the partner.


**Training Restrictions**:
- Practice under qualified supervision with a cooperative partner.
- Do not force the movement through pain or a restricted joint range.
