## How to Recognize This Submission

<p class="section-subtitle">How do you know when someone is attempting Buggy Choke from Buggy Choke?</p>

- Begin underneath side control with your same-side arm and leg forming the Buggy loop around the opponent's head and one arm.
- Confirm the head-and-one-arm enclosure before adding pressure; side-control contact alone is not the choke.
- Keep the raised leg supported by a comfortable hip angle rather than pulling the foot against a fixed knee.

## Common Defensive Mistakes

<p class="section-subtitle">What mistakes should you avoid when defending Buggy Choke from Buggy Choke?</p>

### 1. Acting after the joint or neck is already loaded

- **Consequence**: The attempted movement may increase pressure.
- **Correction**: Tap and release before moving.

### 2. Assuming a cleared grip completes the escape

- **Consequence**: The remaining control can still prevent the intended landing.
- **Correction**: After release, confirm whether the loop remains or side control has returned.
