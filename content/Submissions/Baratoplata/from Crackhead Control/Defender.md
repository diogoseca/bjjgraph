<section id="overview" class="content-section">

You oppose the performer of this action. Keep the elbow compact before the separate arm isolation forms. Maintain an inside frame while the bottom player changes the high clinch. Clear the wrist while the shoulder remains unloaded. If arm isolation fails, the original Crackhead high clinch can still remain. If the high legs open, rebuild posture in the facing closed guard.

Practice cooperatively with controlled movement. Stop at any tap, verbal signal, or distress; release all joint or neck pressure before changing direction. Do not force a trapped knee, shoulder, or neck to preserve the route.

**Opponent's Starting Position**: [[Positions/Rubber Guard/Crackhead Control]] (Bottom)


</section>



<section id="recognition-cues" class="content-section">

## How to Recognize This Submission

<p class="section-subtitle">How do you know when someone is attempting Baratoplata from Crackhead Control?</p>

- Start as the bottom holder of Crackhead Control with the first high leg and second reinforcing leg connected.
- Keep the upper body close while making room to isolate the arm, rather than assuming the high clinch already isolates it.
- Establish the Duda-style arm isolation required for the Baratoplata before changing the leg and grip arrangement.
