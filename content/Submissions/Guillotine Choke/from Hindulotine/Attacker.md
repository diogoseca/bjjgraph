<section id="overview" class="content-section">

The attacker is in front of a lowered head, with the choking arm around the neck and a stable standing or guard connection. Hindu Control includes a raised leg in the enclosure. An ordinary guillotine requires deliberate re-gripping and leg clearance; a shared word in the names does not make the grips interchangeable.

Establish a chin-strap or equivalent front head control without twisting the jaw. Bring the forearm into the neck space while keeping the head aligned. Secure the selected grip without pulling the head backward.

Use a stable base or guard connection so the opponent cannot freely circle around the choke. Use the torso and elbows to remove slack; reset if the pressure becomes a neck crank. Open both hands and remove body pressure immediately on a tap.

Use slow enclosure practice and avoid cervical extension or twisting. Open the hands and remove the neck restraint immediately on any tap.

If the opponent clears your legs to the weak side, stop trying to finish the guillotine. Turn onto your side before a crossface and shoulder pressure pin the upper body; check the near knee or hip with the free hand as hip movement makes room to return your bottom knee inside. Keep the remaining neck connection unloaded while recovering that guard space.

## Key Attacking Principles

<p class="section-subtitle">What are the key principles for executing Guillotine Choke from Hindulotine?</p>

- Control the head: Establish a chin-strap or equivalent front head control without twisting the jaw.
- Place the choking forearm: Bring the forearm into the neck space while keeping the head aligned.
- Connect the support hand: Secure the selected grip without pulling the head backward.
- Control the body relationship: Use a stable base or guard connection so the opponent cannot freely circle around the choke.
- Close space gradually: Use the torso and elbows to remove slack; reset if the pressure becomes a neck crank.
- Release the neck: Open both hands and remove body pressure immediately on a tap.
