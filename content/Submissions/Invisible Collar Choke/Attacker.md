<section id="overview" class="content-section">

The bottom Invisible Collar holder applies the vascular choke from the established high-leg enclosure, using gradual pressure and immediate release without neck cranking.

Begin as the bottom holder of the established Invisible Collar enclosure, facing the opponent. Confirm the high-leg and arm connections before attempting any finishing pressure.

Keep both raised knees supported through the hip angle while maintaining the enclosure. Use only the demonstrated vascular-choke configuration; do not substitute a neck crank when alignment is missing. An interrupted finish may leave Invisible Collar intact; opening the second leg can return to Chill Dog. Stop at a hand tap, foot tap, verbal signal or distress. Remove choke pressure before opening the legs.

Practice cooperatively with controlled movement. Stop at any tap, verbal signal, or distress; release all joint or neck pressure before changing direction. Do not force a trapped knee, shoulder, or neck to preserve the route.

**From Position**: [[Positions/Rubber Guard/Invisible Collar]] (Bottom)

## Key Attacking Principles

<p class="section-subtitle">What are the key principles for executing Invisible Collar Choke?</p>

- Bottom owns the high-leg enclosure: Begin as the bottom holder of the established Invisible Collar enclosure, facing the opponent.

- Hip angle supports both raised knees: Keep both raised knees supported through the hip angle while maintaining the enclosure.

- The high clinch may remain intact: An interrupted finish may leave Invisible Collar intact; opening the second leg can return to Chill Dog.
