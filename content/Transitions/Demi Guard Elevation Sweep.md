<section id="overview" class="content-section">

**The Demi Guard elevation sweep uses the gi lapel-and-foot connection to lift a forward-pressing passer off balance and come on top while freeing the fabric for stabilization.**

The elevation opportunity follows from Demi's knee-line lapel connection and the passer's forward pressure. [Legion's definition](https://legionsandiego.com/the-jiu-jitsu-dictionary/#demi-guard) identifies elevation for a sweep, and [Keenan's curriculum](https://jiujitsux.com/courses/keenans-lapel-encyclopedia/lesson/16-1-introduction-to-the-demi-guard/) lists a basic sweep, a lateral tilt and a Demi X variation. This page describes the general elevation-to-top transition, not the detailed sequence of those separate lessons.

A reversal may finish with the opponent retaining [[Positions/Half Guard]] or [[Positions/Open Guard]]. Free the lapel as needed to stabilize rather than assuming that elevation also passed the legs. If the opponent recovers base, retain Demi or recover a guard before their pressure clears your knees.

**From Position**: [[Positions/Demi Guard]] (Bottom)
**Success Rate**: gi only

## Playing as Attacker

[[Demi Guard Elevation Sweep/Attacker|→ Full Attacker Guide]]

### Key Principles


- Start with the full Demi configuration.

- Read a weight shift before lifting.

- Coordinate collar control with the foot-supported lapel.

- Follow the reversal rather than stopping at elevation.

- Free obstructing fabric and stabilize the real landing.

### Common Mistakes


- **Lifting before the passer's weight shifts**
  - Consequence: You work against a stable base and exhaust the grips.
  - Correction: Recognize the forward loading before committing to elevation.

- **Pulling only with the collar arm**
  - Consequence: The knee line stays supported and the passer can recover posture.
  - Correction: Coordinate the upper-body connection with the loaded foot and lapel.

- **Forcing the sweep against a fixed knee**
  - Consequence: The fabric can bind the joint while the bodies rotate.
  - Correction: Release tension and reset if the leg cannot move comfortably.

## Playing as Defender

[[Demi Guard Elevation Sweep/Defender|→ Full Defender Guide]]

### Key Principles


- Recognize the change of collar, lapel and foot controls early.

- Keep a usable base or leg barrier while contesting the movement.

- Choose a response that fits the current controls rather than a lost grip.

- Release any connection that binds a knee during movement.

- Identify the resulting position before continuing the exchange.


### Recognition Cues


- Your controlled knee begins to rise with the lapel-supported foot.

- The collar connection draws your torso into the changing base.

- The guard player follows the elevation to come up rather than staying flat.


### Defensive Options


- **Recover a stable post and center your weight** - When: Before the elevation carries your hips beyond your base

- **Reduce the foot-on-lapel load while keeping balance** - When: When you feel the controlled knee beginning to lift

- **Clear the legs after the sweep structure collapses** - When: Only after the collar and leg barrier no longer stop your advance
