<section id="overview" class="content-section">

**Worm Guard to Demi Guard establishes a gi lapel guard by arranging collar control, the lapel behind the passer's knee, and a foot on its tail while keeping the legs in front.**

Keenan's introduction places Demi in the response to forward pressure after a worm-wrestling attempt. Once the foot has left the original worm configuration, the guard player may need a different barrier instead of trying to recreate the old wrap under pressure. Start from [[Positions/Worm Guard]] bottom and recognize the passer coming forward as you leave the original leg wrap.

The destination is established only when the collar, knee-line lapel and foot work together. If the feed fails, retain a usable guard instead of holding loose fabric while the opponent clears your legs. The configuration is described in [Legion's dictionary](https://legionsandiego.com/the-jiu-jitsu-dictionary/#demi-guard); [Keenan's introduction](https://jiujitsux.com/courses/keenans-lapel-encyclopedia/lesson/16-1-introduction-to-the-demi-guard/) explains its response to forward pressure.

**From Position**: [[Positions/Worm Guard]] (Bottom)
**Success Rate**: gi only

## Playing as Attacker

[[Worm Guard to Demi Guard/Attacker|→ Full Attacker Guide]]

### Key Principles


- Keep the legs in front during the grip change.

- Arrange the collar control before relying on the lapel barrier.

- Route the lapel behind the knee without twisting the joint.

- Confirm foot contact on the tail before calling the entry complete.

- Retain the starting guard when the feed cannot be completed.

### Common Mistakes


- **Releasing every starting control at once**
  - Consequence: The passer can advance through the unprotected space.
  - Correction: Transfer one connection at a time while keeping the leg barrier.

- **Feeding cloth while your knees are already passed**
  - Consequence: The collar and lapel cannot replace missing guard frames.
  - Correction: Recover your legs before attempting this entry.

- **Placing the foot on slack fabric**
  - Consequence: The foot has no useful connection to the opponent's knee.
  - Correction: Recheck the lapel path and remove only enough slack to make contact work.

## Playing as Defender

[[Worm Guard to Demi Guard/Defender|→ Full Defender Guide]]

### Key Principles


- Recognize the change of collar, lapel and foot controls early.

- Keep a usable base or leg barrier while contesting the movement.

- Choose a response that fits the current controls rather than a lost grip.

- Release any connection that binds a knee during movement.

- Identify the resulting position before continuing the exchange.


### Recognition Cues


- The guard player takes or adjusts a collar grip.

- The lapel is being guided behind your knee.

- A foot approaches the lapel tail as the knees remain between you.


### Defensive Options


- **Contest the feed while preserving base** - When: Before the lapel reaches a useful position behind your knee

- **Prevent the foot from settling on the tail** - When: During the change of leg position

- **Clear the legs during an abandoned feed** - When: Only when the guard player has lost both the lapel connection and the leg barrier
