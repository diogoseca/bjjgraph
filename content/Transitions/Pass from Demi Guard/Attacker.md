<section id="overview" class="content-section">

You begin on top against established Demi Guard. Keep balance while removing the foot-supported lapel connection, then clear the legs and settle side control. Work only through space that is actually available; stop and release tension whenever fabric binds a knee during the change.

**From Position**: [[Positions/Demi Guard]] (Top)

</section>



<section id="key-principles" class="content-section">

## Key Attacking Principles

<p class="section-subtitle">What are the key principles for executing Pass from Demi Guard?</p>

- Base comes before reaching to clear fabric.
- Reduce the foot-supported lapel connection before driving forward.
- Free the knee without twisting against the remaining wrap.
- Check the leg barrier separately from the fabric grip.
- Recognize the actual landing before continuing.


</section>

<section id="setup-requirements" class="content-section">

## Prerequisites

<p class="section-subtitle">What do you need before attempting Pass from Demi Guard?</p>

- You are in Demi Guard top, facing the bottom player.
- You can identify the collar grip and foot-on-lapel connection.
- Your base allows a controlled grip-clearing attempt.
- You can move the knee without forcing it against tightly bound fabric.


</section>

<section id="execution-steps" class="content-section">

## Execution Steps

<p class="section-subtitle">How do you execute Pass from Demi Guard step by step?</p>

1. **Restore a stable base**: Center your weight over a usable base before reaching for the fabric, so the reach itself does not load the opponent's elevation.
2. **Address the upper-body connection**: Contest the collar control without abandoning balance, reducing the pull that brings your torso over the controlled knee.
3. **Reduce the foot-on-lapel connection**: Control the accessible fabric and remove the foot's effective support, using a controlled adjustment rather than a sudden knee rotation.
4. **Free the knee line**: Confirm that the lapel no longer binds the controlled knee before moving the leg through or away from the guard barrier.
5. **Clear the leg barrier**: Guide the remaining legs out of your path while controlling the upper body; if one leg remains trapped, stop at top half guard.
6. **Stabilize side control**: Settle beside the torso only after both legs are cleared, keeping control so the opponent cannot immediately recover a knee inside.


</section>

<section id="common-mistakes" class="content-section hide-minimal">

## Common Attacking Mistakes

<p class="section-subtitle">What mistakes should you avoid when executing Pass from Demi Guard?</p>

### 1. Reaching down before securing base

- **Consequence**: Your weight moves onto the opponent's elevation structure.
- **Correction**: Stabilize your base before the fabric-clearing reach.

### 2. Driving forward while the foot remains supported

- **Consequence**: The same pressure can increase the bottom player's sweep opportunity.
- **Correction**: Reduce the foot-on-lapel connection before applying passing pressure.

### 3. Rotating the knee against taut fabric

- **Consequence**: The lapel restricts the leg during the rotation.
- **Correction**: Release the binding tension before moving the knee.

### 4. Ignoring a surviving half-guard trap

- **Consequence**: Your passing movement stalls while one leg remains captured.
- **Correction**: Recognize half guard and address its controls before progressing.

### 5. Treating grip clearance as full positional control

- **Consequence**: The bottom player can keep or recover ordinary guard frames.
- **Correction**: Check fabric, legs and upper-body control as separate tasks.



</section>

<section id="training-progressions" class="content-section hide-minimal">

## Training Progressions

<p class="section-subtitle">How do you train Pass from Demi Guard (Attacker)?</p>

**Recognition** - Identify the starting configuration
You are in Demi Guard top, facing the bottom player. With a cooperative partner, identify the required grips and leg contacts, then reset without applying pressure.

**Cooperative sequence** - Practice the control change
Perform the listed sequence slowly with a cooperative partner. Pause at the change of lapel or foot control and reset on any joint discomfort.

**One response** - Recognize the first defense
Let the partner use this response: Restore the collar and foot connection. Practice keeping a safe base or guard when it blocks the attempt, then reset.

**Bounded exchange** - Confirm the landing
Start at the stated origin with light resistance. Stop and reset at a stable destination, a counter, or a request to release; do not continue through a bound knee.



</section>



<section id="safety-considerations" class="content-section">

## Safety Considerations

<p class="section-subtitle">What are the safety concerns for Pass from Demi Guard?</p>

Use controlled pressure with a cooperative partner when learning the lapel path. Release fabric immediately on a tap, verbal stop or discomfort. Do not rotate against a knee held by the lapel, and stop the drill when a stable landing is reached.
