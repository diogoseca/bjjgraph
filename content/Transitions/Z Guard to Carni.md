From bottom Z-Guard, the Carni entry passes through the Jersey shoulder connection, clears the high leg around the head, and establishes the upper-arm clamp before finishing.

Begin underneath in Z-Guard with a stable knee-shield connection and access to the opponent's shoulder. Use the Jersey intermediate taught with this entry rather than treating knee-shield contact as a completed Carni.

Use the frame and hip angle to give the high leg a clear path around the head without pulling on a fixed knee. Move the high leg through the available space while keeping the opposing arm close. Hold the upper arm at the triceps as the leg and torso angle establish the shoulder enclosure. The entry succeeds at Carni/Bottom. Wrist leverage and finishing pressure belong to the separate shoulder-lock action.

Practice cooperatively with controlled movement. Stop at any tap, verbal signal, or distress; release all joint or neck pressure before changing direction. Do not force a trapped knee, shoulder, or neck to preserve the route.

## Playing as Defender

[[Z Guard to Carni/Defender|→ Full Defender Guide]]

### Key Principles


- Keep a useful upper-body frame before the shoulder connection closes.

- Prevent the upper arm from separating from the torso while Jersey control is forming.

- Clear the arm before the high leg closes around the shoulder.

- If the entry stalls, maintain your base above the remaining Z-Guard.

- If the high connection opens fully, recover posture inside the ordinary closed guard.
