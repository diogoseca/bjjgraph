<section id="overview" class="content-section">

You begin in Demi Guard bottom and keep a useful lapel grip as the passer moves away from the close pressure exchange. Replace the foot-on-tail configuration with ordinary leg frames while staying in front. The aim is a usable lapel guard from which a separate attack can be arranged.

**From Position**: [[Positions/Demi Guard]] (Bottom)

</section>



<section id="key-principles" class="content-section">

## Key Attacking Principles

<p class="section-subtitle">What are the key principles for executing Demi Guard to Lapel Guard?</p>

- Keep useful fabric control during the change.
- Use the space created by the passer's retreat.
- Replace foot contact with a working leg barrier.
- Stay facing the passer while changing the connection.
- Treat a later attack as a separate setup.


</section>

<section id="setup-requirements" class="content-section">

## Prerequisites

<p class="section-subtitle">What do you need before attempting Demi Guard to Lapel Guard?</p>

- You are in Demi Guard bottom.
- The passer has created enough distance to change the foot configuration.
- A useful lapel grip remains available.
- Your legs can remain between the two players during the change.


</section>

<section id="execution-steps" class="content-section">

## Execution Steps

<p class="section-subtitle">How do you execute Demi Guard to Lapel Guard step by step?</p>

1. **Read the distance change**: Recognize that the passer has reduced forward pressure and made room to change the close foot-on-lapel configuration.
2. **Preserve a usable grip**: Keep control of the lapel without pulling it so tightly that it prevents the leg movement needed for guard recovery.
3. **Replace the foot barrier**: Move the foot off the tail only while arranging a leg frame that keeps the passer from advancing directly to your torso.
4. **Face and follow the passer**: Adjust your hip angle so your knees remain between the players as the passer changes distance or direction.
5. **Settle general lapel control**: Confirm that you have usable fabric control and a guard barrier without requiring the defining Demi foot-on-tail placement.
6. **Prepare a separate follow-up**: Choose a lapel-guard sweep or submission setup only after confirming its additional grips and leg position in the recovered guard.


</section>

<section id="common-mistakes" class="content-section hide-minimal">

## Common Attacking Mistakes

<p class="section-subtitle">What mistakes should you avoid when executing Demi Guard to Lapel Guard?</p>

### 1. Dropping the lapel while changing the foot

- **Consequence**: The intended lapel-guard continuation loses its main control.
- **Correction**: Retain a useful grip while replacing the leg barrier.

### 2. Removing the foot without a replacement frame

- **Consequence**: The passer can close the gap directly to your torso.
- **Correction**: Arrange another guard frame during the foot change.

### 3. Keeping the old wrap tighter than the movement permits

- **Consequence**: Your own leg cannot move into the recovered guard.
- **Correction**: Release only the tension that obstructs the change.

### 4. Letting the passer circle behind your knees

- **Consequence**: The fabric grip no longer protects a viable guard angle.
- **Correction**: Follow with your hips to keep the knees between the players.

### 5. Chasing a back take before recovering guard

- **Consequence**: Missing controls expose the guard change to a pass.
- **Correction**: Establish the destination and then meet the next technique's setup requirements.



</section>

<section id="training-progressions" class="content-section hide-minimal">

## Training Progressions

<p class="section-subtitle">How do you train Demi Guard to Lapel Guard (Attacker)?</p>

**Recognition** - Identify the starting configuration
You are in Demi Guard bottom. With a cooperative partner, identify the required grips and leg contacts, then reset without applying pressure.

**Cooperative sequence** - Practice the control change
Perform the listed sequence slowly with a cooperative partner. Pause at the change of lapel or foot control and reset on any joint discomfort.

**One response** - Recognize the first defense
Let the partner use this response: Keep the existing configuration contained. Practice keeping a safe base or guard when it blocks the attempt, then reset.

**Bounded exchange** - Confirm the landing
Start at the stated origin with light resistance. Stop and reset at a stable destination, a counter, or a request to release; do not continue through a bound knee.



</section>



<section id="safety-considerations" class="content-section">

## Safety Considerations

<p class="section-subtitle">What are the safety concerns for Demi Guard to Lapel Guard?</p>

Use controlled pressure with a cooperative partner when learning the lapel path. Release fabric immediately on a tap, verbal stop or discomfort. Do not rotate against a knee held by the lapel, and stop the drill when a stable landing is reached.
