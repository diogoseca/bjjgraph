The bottom Chill Dog holder uses the cleared-neck angle to establish a shin-under-chin Gogoplata enclosure, stopping at controlled setup before any separate finish.

Start as the bottom player after the neck has been cleared into the Chill Dog configuration. Maintain a comfortable hip angle to support the raised leg throughout the exchange.

Preserve the space created by neck clearance before bringing the shin toward the chin. Establish the shin-under-chin connection without forcing the knee or adding uncontrolled neck pressure. The destination is Gogoplata Control/Bottom only when that distinct enclosure is present. Stop at the control checkpoint; finishing pressure belongs to the separate submission action.

Practice cooperatively with controlled movement. Stop at any tap, verbal signal, or distress; release all joint or neck pressure before changing direction. Do not force a trapped knee, shoulder, or neck to preserve the route.
