<section id="overview" class="content-section">

You oppose the performer of this action. Keep the hand connected to useful inside space while the pummel is contested. Address the hand clearance before the knee hug closes. Maintain a balanced posture without forcing the raised knee. Recognize an unfinished pummel as Zombie rather than completed New York. Rebuild inside hand position if the holder returns to Mission Control.

Practice cooperatively with controlled movement. Stop at any tap, verbal signal, or distress; release all joint or neck pressure before changing direction. Do not force a trapped knee, shoulder, or neck to preserve the route.

**Opponent's Starting Position**: [[Positions/Rubber Guard/Zombie]] (Bottom)

</section>

## How to Recognize This Attack

<p class="section-subtitle">How do you know when someone is attempting Zombie to New York?</p>

## Common Defensive Mistakes

<p class="section-subtitle">What mistakes should you avoid when defending Zombie to New York?</p>

### 1. Acting after the joint or neck is already loaded

- **Consequence**: The attempted movement may increase pressure.
- **Correction**: Tap and release before moving.

### 2. Assuming a cleared grip completes the escape

- **Consequence**: The remaining control can still prevent the intended landing.
- **Correction**: Rebuild inside hand position if the holder returns to Mission Control.

### 3. Losing a stable base during the response

- **Consequence**: You may concede the next connection.
- **Correction**: Address the hand clearance before the knee hug closes.
