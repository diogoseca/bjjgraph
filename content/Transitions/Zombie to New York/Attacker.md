<section id="overview" class="content-section">

The bottom Zombie player completes the contested hand-clearing action, places the hand on the mat, and establishes the knee hug that defines completed New York control.

Begin in the Zombie hand-clearing checkpoint, before completed New York control has been secured. Retain the first high-leg connection while the opposing hand is being cleared.

Complete the hand-clearing action rather than treating a partially displaced hand as success. Establish the knee-hug connection after the hand reaches the mat. The destination is New York/Bottom only after both the hand-to-mat and knee-hug checkpoints exist. If the hand-clearing connection is lost, recognize the retained Mission Control configuration before starting again.

Practice cooperatively with controlled movement. Stop at any tap, verbal signal, or distress; release all joint or neck pressure before changing direction. Do not force a trapped knee, shoulder, or neck to preserve the route.

**From Position**: [[Positions/Rubber Guard/Zombie]] (Bottom)
