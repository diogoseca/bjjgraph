**The Buggy loop holder releases the choke, restores inside frames, and inserts a knee to recover bottom half guard while keeping the neck and raised knee unloaded.**

The Buggy loop holder releases the choke, restores inside frames, and inserts a knee to recover bottom half guard while keeping the neck and raised knee unloaded.

Begin underneath the opponent with your arm-and-leg Buggy enclosure still present. Remove the choke pressure before opening the enclosure or asking the partner to change direction.

Release the loop through a comfortable hip angle without pulling the raised knee against resistance. Place inside frames as the enclosure opens to preserve space between the bodies. Use the framed space to bring a knee inside and establish the required half-guard leg connection. The recovery is Half Guard/Bottom only once the leg connection exists; losing the loop without it leaves Side Control/Bottom.

Practice cooperatively with controlled movement. Stop at any tap, verbal signal, or distress; release all joint or neck pressure before changing direction. Do not force a trapped knee, shoulder, or neck to preserve the route.
