<section id="overview" class="content-section">

This gi-only leg-weave counter uses sleeve and far-trouser grips to reverse the passer, then settles top half guard instead of assuming an additional guard pass.

Begin underneath the passer's arm weave; retain enough space to establish the cloth grips used by this counter. Secure the sleeve so the passer cannot freely post that hand in the direction of the reversal.

Establish the far-trouser connection. This cloth-dependent version has no modeled no-gi counterpart. Coordinate the upper- and lower-body controls to move the passer's base while maintaining safe knee alignment. Follow the turnover with a stable base rather than releasing the controls before your weight comes above the opponent. The approved completed destination is Half Guard/Top. Establishing side control would require a separate pass.

Practice cooperatively with controlled movement. Stop at any tap, verbal signal, or distress; release all joint or neck pressure before changing direction. Do not force a trapped knee, shoulder, or neck to preserve the route.

**From Position**: [[Positions/Leg Weave]] (Bottom)

- Come on top with the leg retained: Follow the turnover with a stable base rather than releasing the controls before your weight comes above the opponent.
- Top half guard before passing: The approved completed destination is Half Guard/Top. Establishing side control would require a separate pass.
