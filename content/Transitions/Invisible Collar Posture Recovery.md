**The top player opens the high Invisible Collar enclosure before recovering posture, then identifies closed or open guard with the head and shoulders safely released.**

The top player opens the high Invisible Collar enclosure before recovering posture, then identifies closed or open guard with the head and shoulders safely released.

Begin as the top player caught by the bottom holder's high Invisible Collar leg enclosure. Keep the head and shoulders aligned instead of forcing posture through a closed enclosure.

Release the high enclosure while respecting the position of the head, arm and raised knees. Clear the shoulder line before trying to rebuild upright posture. When ordinary guard closes, recognize Closed Guard/Top with the high enclosure removed. When the legs cannot close, recognize facing Open Guard/Top; an intact high enclosure remains a failed recovery.

Practice cooperatively with controlled movement. Stop at any tap, verbal signal, or distress; release all joint or neck pressure before changing direction. Do not force a trapped knee, shoulder, or neck to preserve the route.

**From Position**: [[Positions/Rubber Guard/Invisible Collar]] (Top)
**Success Rate**: 30%

## Playing as Defender

[[Invisible Collar Posture Recovery/Defender|→ Full Defender Guide]]

### Key Principles


- Retain the high enclosure without forcing neck pressure.

- Follow small posture changes with a supported hip angle.

- Keep the first connection if the second leg begins to open.

- Restore closed guard when the high enclosure releases and distance permits.

- Use open guard if the ankles cannot close comfortably.
