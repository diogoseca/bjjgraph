**The top player releases the head-and-leg enclosure of Hindu Control early, then restores posture in closed or open guard without forcing a neck twist or assuming a pass.**

The top player releases the head-and-leg enclosure of Hindu Control early, then restores posture in closed or open guard without forcing a neck twist or assuming a pass.

Begin as the top player facing the bottom Hindu Control holder, with the head-and-leg enclosure still present. Protect head and neck alignment; tap instead of rotating against established pressure.

Work on releasing the enclosure while the head and leg can still move without forced joint pressure. Free the head from the enclosure before rebuilding upright posture. If the opponent closes ordinary guard, recognize Closed Guard/Top with the Hindu enclosure released. If the ankles do not close, remain facing Open Guard/Top; retained Hindu Control means the release is incomplete.

Practice cooperatively with controlled movement. Stop at any tap, verbal signal, or distress; release all joint or neck pressure before changing direction. Do not force a trapped knee, shoulder, or neck to preserve the route.

**From Position**: [[Positions/Hindulotine]] (Top)
**Success Rate**: 35%
