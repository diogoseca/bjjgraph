<section id="overview" class="content-section">

You begin on top while the worm-guard player starts removing the foot from the wrap to come up. Close the distance that their stand-up needs while maintaining base. If they settle into Demi underneath, the get-up has been contained but the foot-on-lapel elevation threat must now be addressed.

**From Position**: [[Positions/Worm Guard]] (Top)

</section>



<section id="key-principles" class="content-section">

## Key Attacking Principles

<p class="section-subtitle">What are the key principles for executing Worm Wrestling Pressure Counter?</p>

- Recognize the foot leaving the original worm wrap.
- Respond to the attempt to come up rather than pressuring a static wrap.
- Close distance with a stable base.
- Recognize the replacement Demi connection immediately.
- Separate containing a stand-up from passing the guard.


</section>

<section id="setup-requirements" class="content-section">

## Prerequisites

<p class="section-subtitle">What do you need before attempting Worm Wrestling Pressure Counter?</p>

- You are in Worm Guard top.
- The bottom player is beginning to leave the original wrap for a worm-wrestling get-up.
- You can close distance without forcing a lapel-bound knee.
- You retain enough base to respond to a change of guard.


</section>

<section id="execution-steps" class="content-section">

## Execution Steps

<p class="section-subtitle">How do you execute Worm Wrestling Pressure Counter step by step?</p>

1. **Recognize the get-up**: Watch for the foot leaving the worm configuration as the bottom player begins coming toward their knees or feet.
2. **Keep the knee free to move**: Check that the changing wrap permits movement before stepping in; stop if the lapel still binds your knee.
3. **Close the available distance**: Move forward with your base beneath you to occupy the space the guard player needs for the attempted stand-up.
4. **Contain the rise**: Maintain a facing top position as the bottom player returns their legs in front instead of completing the get-up.
5. **Recognize Demi Guard**: Identify the collar grip, lapel behind your knee and foot on the tail if the opponent assembles that replacement guard.
6. **Reset the passing priority**: Stay balanced and address the new lapel barrier before further pressure; containing the rise has not cleared their legs.


</section>

<section id="common-counters" class="content-section">

## Opponent Counters

<p class="section-subtitle">How might your opponent counter Worm Wrestling Pressure Counter?</p>

- **The bottom player retains the original worm wrap** (Effectiveness: Medium) - Your Response: Do not force the forward step through an intact wrap; reassess the original guard controls. → Leads to [[Positions/Worm Guard]]
- **The bottom player denies the distance change** (Effectiveness: Medium) - Your Response: Keep your base and remain engaged rather than reaching beyond your balance. → Leads to [[Positions/Worm Guard]]
- **The bottom player redirects the committed pressure into a reversal** (Effectiveness: Medium) - Your Response: Release binding fabric and recover guard as you land underneath. → Leads to [[Positions/Half Guard]]


</section>

<section id="common-mistakes" class="content-section hide-minimal">

## Common Attacking Mistakes

<p class="section-subtitle">What mistakes should you avoid when executing Worm Wrestling Pressure Counter?</p>

### 1. Applying this response before the foot leaves the wrap

- **Consequence**: The original worm connection still restricts the moving leg.
- **Correction**: Wait for the actual get-up transition and confirm that the knee can move.

### 2. Reaching forward without moving the base

- **Consequence**: The bottom player can load your unsupported weight.
- **Correction**: Keep the base beneath the torso while closing distance.

### 3. Ignoring the newly established Demi foot

- **Consequence**: The replacement guard can elevate you through the lapel.
- **Correction**: Recognize the foot-on-tail connection and stop driving blindly.

### 4. Treating containment as a completed guard pass

- **Consequence**: The opponent still has legs and lapel controls between the bodies.
- **Correction**: Address the resulting guard before attempting to settle a pin.

### 5. Turning through a lapel-bound knee

- **Consequence**: The joint cannot follow the body freely.
- **Correction**: Release the binding tension and reset the movement.



</section>

<section id="training-progressions" class="content-section hide-minimal">

## Training Progressions

<p class="section-subtitle">How do you train Worm Wrestling Pressure Counter (Attacker)?</p>

**Recognition** - Identify the get-up transition
Start in cooperative Worm Guard. The bottom player begins removing the foot from the wrap; name that cue and reset.

**Controlled engagement** - Close distance with base
Let the bottom player begin a slow get-up. Practice closing the available space without pressing through a bound knee; stop and reset.

**Guard recognition** - Identify the replacement structure
Allow the bottom player to settle into Demi after the pressure response. Name the collar, knee-line lapel and foot before releasing.

**Bounded exchange** - Contain or recover
Use light resistance from the get-up attempt. Stop at a stable guard, a completed rise or a reversal; release immediately on discomfort.



</section>



<section id="safety-considerations" class="content-section">

## Safety Considerations

<p class="section-subtitle">What are the safety concerns for Worm Wrestling Pressure Counter?</p>

Use controlled forward movement during cooperative practice. Do not step or turn through a knee that is still bound by the lapel. Release immediately on a tap, verbal stop or discomfort, and reset once the resulting guard is established.
