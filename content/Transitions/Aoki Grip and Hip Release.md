**The caught player releases the Aoki foot grip early, frees the hip connection, and confirms top Ashi Garami or facing open guard before attempting another action.**

The caught player releases the Aoki foot grip early, frees the hip connection, and confirms top Ashi Garami or facing open guard before attempting another action.

Begin as the caught player in Aoki Lock Control/Top; the opposing player controls your foot from underneath. Work only while the foot and knee remain unloaded. Tap instead of turning against established joint pressure.

Address the foot grip before trying to rotate the hips through the retained leg connection. Move the hip only after the foot is free to follow without twisting against the grip. If the leg entanglement remains, recognize Ashi Garami/Top rather than claiming complete disengagement. If the entanglement also clears, remain facing the opponent in Open Guard/Top. A failed release remains Aoki Lock Control.

Practice cooperatively with controlled movement. Stop at any tap, verbal signal, or distress; release all joint or neck pressure before changing direction. Do not force a trapped knee, shoulder, or neck to preserve the route.

**From Position**: [[Positions/Aoki Lock Control]] (Top)
**Success Rate**: 35%

## Playing as Defender

[[Aoki Grip and Hip Release/Defender|→ Full Defender Guide]]

### Key Principles


- Retain the foot connection without forcing rotation.

- Follow the early hip movement with a supported leg connection.

- Release immediately if the partner taps or signals discomfort.

- Recognize Ashi Garami if the foot grip opens but the legs remain connected.

- Recognize facing open guard if both connections clear.
