From Crackhead Control, the bottom player uses the double-bagger sequence to bring both opposing arms inside a high leg triangle and establish Dead Orchard.

Begin as the bottom player with Crackhead's second leg reinforcing the first high-leg clinch. Keep the knees supported by the hip angle; do not pull a foot against a fixed knee to make the transition.

Use the double-bagger sequence to organize both high-leg connections while keeping the opponent close. Bring both opposing arms into the high enclosure before closing the triangle. Form the high leg triangle around the shoulder line with both arms contained and no finishing pressure applied. The success is Dead Orchard Control/Bottom. An armbar requires its own wrist and elbow control after this checkpoint.

Practice cooperatively with controlled movement. Stop at any tap, verbal signal, or distress; release all joint or neck pressure before changing direction. Do not force a trapped knee, shoulder, or neck to preserve the route.

## Playing as Defender

[[Crackhead Control to Dead Orchard/Defender|→ Full Defender Guide]]

### Key Principles


- Keep your elbows compact before both arms enter the high legs.

- Maintain an inside frame before the second leg closes the enclosure.

- Move shoulder and elbow together while the leg connection is still open.
