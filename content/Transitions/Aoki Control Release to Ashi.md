The Aoki holder removes finishing pressure and opens the foot grip while retaining the Ashi Garami leg connection, returning to control before choosing a new attack.

Begin as the holder in Aoki Lock Control/Bottom with the foot grip and the leg connection established. Remove the finishing pressure before allowing the captured foot to change angle.

Open the foot grip without pulling the foot through a fixed knee angle. Keep the established Ashi Garami leg connection while the foot grip is released. The completed reset is Ashi Garami/Bottom, with no foot-lock finishing pressure. If the leg connection also opens, recognize Open Guard/Bottom before choosing another attack.

Practice cooperatively with controlled movement. Stop at any tap, verbal signal, or distress; release all joint or neck pressure before changing direction. Do not force a trapped knee, shoulder, or neck to preserve the route.
