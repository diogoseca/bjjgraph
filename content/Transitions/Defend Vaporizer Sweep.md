<section id="overview" class="content-section">

Defending the Vaporizer sweep is a critical survival skill for practitioners caught in the 10th Planet lockdown system. When the bottom player establishes the Vaporizer position with a deep lockdown and aggressive whip-up motion, the top player faces immediate sweep threats including the Old School sweep, rolling back takes, and transitions to the Electric Chair or Truck. This defensive transition focuses on neutralizing sweep mechanics while systematically working to extract from the lockdown and return to a standard half guard passing position.

The defense operates on the principle of base management and pressure redistribution. Rather than fighting the lockdown directly with explosive force, the top player must widen their base, lower their center of gravity, and apply strategic shoulder pressure to counter the whip-up motion that generates sweep momentum. The key technical challenge lies in simultaneously addressing both the lower body entanglement and the upper body control that the bottom player uses as complementary attack vectors.

Successful execution requires reading the bottom player's specific sweep attempt and applying the appropriate counter at the right moment. Against the Old School sweep, the defender must prevent the posted foot from generating leverage. Against rolling attacks toward the Truck, the defender must control hip rotation. This situational awareness, combined with methodical lockdown clearing technique, transforms a desperate defensive scramble into a systematic positional recovery that feeds directly into standard half guard passing sequences.

### Key Principles


- Widen base immediately upon detecting whip-up motion by sprawling the free leg laterally to create a stable platform resistant to rolling forces

- Apply heavy chest-to-chest shoulder pressure into the bottom player to limit their hip mobility and reduce whip-up effectiveness

- Address the lockdown through controlled leg circulation and knee positioning rather than explosive extraction that tightens the entanglement

- Counter the underhook with a whizzer or crossface to neutralize the upper body leverage essential for sweep completion

- Distribute weight to simultaneously defend against both the Old School sweep direction and the rolling back take direction

- Maintain patient grinding defensive pressure rather than explosive movements that waste energy and create space for the bottom player's attacks

### Common Mistakes


- **Attempting to pull the trapped leg free with explosive force rather than controlled circulation**
  - Consequence: Creates space and energy expenditure that the bottom player exploits to tighten the lockdown, complete the sweep, or advance to Electric Chair
  - Correction: Use controlled leg circulation by pointing knee to mat, rotating foot in small circles, and gradually working heel toward buttock while maintaining shoulder pressure throughout

- **Maintaining a narrow base with knees close together during sweep defense**
  - Consequence: Bottom player easily generates the rotational momentum needed to complete Old School sweep or rolling back take due to minimal lateral stability
  - Correction: Sprawl free leg wide immediately upon detecting whip-up pressure, creating a tripod base that resists lateral forces from multiple directions

- **Posting hand on the mat near the bottom player's hip during whip-up defense**
  - Consequence: Creates a perfect lever point for Old School sweep completion and may expose the arm to underhook attacks or Kimura threats
  - Correction: Post hands wide and away from the bottom player's hip, or keep hands on their body for control rather than on the mat where they become leverage points

## Playing as Defender

[[Defend Vaporizer Sweep/Defender|→ Full Defender Guide]]

### Key Principles


- Read the top player's defensive response and immediately transition to the attack that exploits their specific positional commitment

- Maintain lockdown control throughout the defensive exchange as the primary anchor for all offensive options from the Vaporizer

- Use the top player's defensive weight distribution against them by threatening attacks in the direction opposite their defensive commitment

- Chain sweep and submission attempts in rapid sequence rather than fully committing to a single technique the top player has identified and is defending

- Keep whip-up pressure active even during defended sequences to prevent the top player from establishing a settled defensive base

- Recognize when the top player begins systematic lockdown clearing and either accelerate attacks or tighten the lockdown before extraction progresses


### Recognition Cues


- Top player widens base and sprawls free leg laterally, indicating they have identified the sweep direction and are establishing defensive stability against lateral rolling

- Top player drives heavy shoulder pressure into your chest while maintaining low hips, signaling direct counter to the whip-up mechanism

- Top player begins circulating trapped leg or pointing knee toward the mat, indicating active lockdown clearing attempt that will eventually free their leg

- Top player establishes whizzer or crossface against your underhook, showing systematic defense of the upper body control essential for sweep completion

- Top player's breathing becomes controlled and movements become deliberate rather than explosive, indicating a patient defensive strategy designed to avoid creating exploitable space


### Defensive Options


- **Transition to Electric Chair by releasing lockdown and controlling far leg when top player widens base to defend sweep** - When: When the top player commits to a wide base with knees spread apart to resist lateral rolling, exposing the far leg to capture and split

- **Switch to rolling back take toward Truck when top player drives forward shoulder pressure** - When: When the top player commits weight forward through shoulder pressure, creating momentum that can be redirected into a rotational back take toward Truck position

- **Tighten lockdown and re-establish whip-up from an adjusted angle after initial defense** - When: When the top player has defended the initial sweep but has not yet begun clearing the lockdown, providing a window to reset the attacking sequence

<section id="variants" class="content-section hide-minimal">

## Variations


**Base Widening Defense**: Primary defensive response focused on sprawling the free leg wide and lowering the center of gravity to create maximum resistance against lateral rolling forces. Emphasizes stability and pressure over active escape. (When to use: When the bottom player has strong whip-up pressure and is threatening the Old School sweep with a posted foot and active underhook driving lateral rotation)


**Pressure Through Defense**: Aggressive defensive response that drives heavy shoulder pressure into the bottom player's chest to pin them flat and deny the hip mobility needed for effective whip-up motion. Trades mobility for crushing top pressure. (When to use: When the bottom player's lockdown is moderate and their primary threat comes from upper body leverage rather than leg entanglement power)


**Active Lockdown Clearing Defense**: Combines sweep defense with immediate lockdown extraction using controlled leg circulation, knee pointing, and gradual heel retraction. Prioritizes escaping the entire Vaporizer position rather than simply surviving the sweep. (When to use: When the bottom player's sweep attempt has been initially neutralized and there is a window to work on extracting the trapped leg before they can re-establish offensive pressure)

## Position Integration

The Defend Vaporizer Sweep transition is essential for any practitioner regularly facing 10th Planet-style half guard players. It connects directly to the broader half guard top passing system, as successful defense feeds into standard passing sequences including the knee slice, smash pass, and pressure passing options. This technique also integrates with the counter-offensive framework from half guard top, where defending a sweep creates opportunities for submissions like the Kimura or Darce Choke when the bottom player overcommits to sweep mechanics. Understanding this defense completes the top player's defensive toolkit against lockdown-based half guard systems and prevents a common positional collapse that leads to being swept or submitted.
