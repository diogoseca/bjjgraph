The bottom Chill Dog holder closes the second high leg after neck clearance and arm isolation, establishing Invisible Collar while keeping both raised knees supported.

Begin as the bottom Chill Dog holder after the neck-clearing step has been completed. Maintain the isolated-arm checkpoint before introducing the second high leg.

Keep the first raised leg supported by the hip angle while preserving the cleared-neck connection. Bring the second high leg into the enclosure without pulling either foot against a fixed knee. Confirm the distinct two-leg Invisible Collar enclosure as the bottom holder. If the second leg cannot close, recognize the remaining Chill Dog configuration before choosing another action.

Practice cooperatively with controlled movement. Stop at any tap, verbal signal, or distress; release all joint or neck pressure before changing direction. Do not force a trapped knee, shoulder, or neck to preserve the route.

**From Position**: [[Positions/Rubber Guard/Chill Dog]] (Bottom)
**Success Rate**: 50%

## Playing as Defender

[[Chill Dog to Invisible Collar/Defender|→ Full Defender Guide]]

### Key Principles


- Keep an inside frame before the second leg closes.

- Keep the isolated arm connected to the torso while space remains.

- Avoid rotating against a fixed head or shoulder.

- Recognize Chill Dog if only the second high-leg connection is interrupted.

- Recover posture in ordinary guard if the high enclosure fully releases.
