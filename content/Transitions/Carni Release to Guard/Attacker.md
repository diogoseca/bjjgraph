<section id="overview" class="content-section">

The Carni holder releases wrist and shoulder pressure, opens the leg-assisted arm clamp, and restores a facing guard with inside frames and comfortable knee alignment.

Begin as the player applying Carni, with the opponent's upper arm and shoulder captured by your leg connection. Stop the submission loading before letting the opponent's arm move through the enclosure.

Support the knee and move through the hip to open the clamp; do not wrench the foot to free the arm. Retain a forearm barrier as the shoulder is released so you can remain facing the opponent. Bring the knees into a comfortable guard shape as the high-leg connection opens. Close ordinary guard when the distance permits. If it does not, maintain facing open guard rather than forcing the ankles together.

Practice cooperatively with controlled movement. Stop at any tap, verbal signal, or distress; release all joint or neck pressure before changing direction. Do not force a trapped knee, shoulder, or neck to preserve the route.

**From Position**: [[Positions/Rubber Guard/Carni]] (Bottom)

</section>

## Key Attacking Principles

<p class="section-subtitle">What are the key principles for executing Carni Release to Guard?</p>

- Knees return between the torsos: Bring the knees into a comfortable guard shape as the high-leg connection opens.
