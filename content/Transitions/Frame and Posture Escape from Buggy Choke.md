The caught top player uses inside frames to open the Buggy loop, clears the head and arm, and disengages into facing open guard after restoring safe head and torso alignment.

Begin as the physically overlying player caught in the bottom player's same-side arm-and-leg loop. Use the available inside frames to make space around the enclosure while protecting the neck.

Recover head and torso alignment without treating improved posture inside a closed loop as a completed escape. Open the enclosure and clear the head and trapped arm before attempting to disengage. Withdraw continuously and square up toward the opponent's legs, conceding the side pin. Recognize Open Guard/Top only after the loop is free. A caught triangle remains Triangle Control/Top, with the same physical actor.

Practice cooperatively with controlled movement. Stop at any tap, verbal signal, or distress; release all joint or neck pressure before changing direction. Do not force a trapped knee, shoulder, or neck to preserve the route.
