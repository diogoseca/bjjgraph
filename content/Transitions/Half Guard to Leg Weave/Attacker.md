<section id="overview" class="content-section">

The top passer begins in Half Guard, facing a knee shield or linked leg frame. The weaving limb is the arm. Securing that control is the entry checkpoint; a pass is complete only after the hips and remaining leg hooks are cleared.

Thread the arm through the available leg gap and connect to the lower thigh or knee. Use aligned shoulder pressure and chest pressure to bring the knees together without twisting the knee joint. Avoid leaving the weaving arm extended where the bottom player can sit up into a kimura.

Move the hips and feet without collapsing all weight over the woven arm. Remove the remaining foot or half-guard hook before establishing side control. If the bottom player turns or inserts a frame, keep control of distance and reset the pass rather than forcing a fixed angle.

Move through available space with a stable base. Stop and release any trapped neck, shoulder or knee before changing direction; do not force a roll or stack to preserve the route.

Pair the weaving arm with head position and control of the opposing elbow. This hip control limits the knees following as you start to circle; reaching for a crossface before the weave is stable gives up the head-and-arm position needed to keep the shield folded.
