# Partnership & Sponsorship

BJJGraph welcomes partnerships with BJJ apps, gear companies, training platforms, and academies.

## Placement Options

### Banner Placements
- **Header banner**: Sitewide visibility on all 267+ pages
- **Footer placement**: Persistent brand exposure
- **Sidebar ads**: Desktop-only prominent positioning

### Contextual Integration
- **Technique pages**: Relevant product mentions (e.g., gi recommendations on guard pages)
- **Position pages**: Training equipment suggestions
- **System pages**: Course/instructional affiliate links

### Dedicated Content
- **Sponsored guides**: Co-branded technique breakdowns
- **Product reviews**: Honest assessments with affiliate tracking
- **Academy features**: Highlighted gym partnerships

## Tracking & Transparency

All affiliate relationships use transparent tracking:
- **Affiliate platform**: Rewardful or similar
- **Clear disclosure**: All sponsored content marked appropriately
- **Performance reporting**: Partners receive traffic and conversion data

## Revenue Sharing

We're flexible on terms and prefer to discuss arrangements directly. Typical structures include:
- Flat monthly sponsorship fees
- Performance-based affiliate commissions (10-30%)
- Hybrid models combining both approaches

Revenue helps maintain the site, improve content quality, and fund development of interactive tools.

## Getting Started

Interested in partnering? Here's the process:

1. **Reach out**: Email or open a GitHub discussion
2. **Share goals**: What are you looking to achieve?
3. **Review fit**: We'll assess alignment with our audience
4. **Agree terms**: Flexible arrangements based on partnership type
5. **Launch**: Implement tracking and begin collaboration
