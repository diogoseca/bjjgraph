Microsoft AI Search Says: ❌ NO TABS

Direct quote:
"Don't hide important answers in tabs or expandable menus: AI 
systems may not render hidden content, so key details can be 
skipped."

Tabs would hurt SEO because:
- AI only sees default tab content
- Non-default tab (Bottom or Top) becomes invisible to AI
- Hurts ranking for escape-focused queries if Bottom is hidden
