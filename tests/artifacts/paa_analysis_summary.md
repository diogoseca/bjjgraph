**Target Query:** "What is backside 50-50 in BJJ?"

**Optimized Answer Format (40-60 words):**
> Backside 50-50 is a BJJ leg entanglement where both practitioners maintain mirrored leg positioning, but one player's back faces the opponent's chest rather than face-to-face. This creates distinct mechanical advantages for the top player through superior pressure and visual access to the opponent's legs.

**Implementation:** Place at start of overview section with H2 heading matching exact query.

---

**Target Query:** "How do you escape backside 50-50?"

**Optimized Answer Format (List for Snippet):**
1. Maintain constant hip mobility to prevent flattening
2. Use frames to create separation from chest pressure
3. Rotate to face opponent (convert to standard 50-50)
4. Rolling back take when opponent overcommits to leg attacks
5. Extract to standing position when opponent loses base

**Implementation:** Create dedicated "How to Escape" section with numbered list.
